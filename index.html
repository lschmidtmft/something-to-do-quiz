What’s Your “Something to Do”? – Based on 1 Corinthians 12:7

Each person is given something to do that shows who God is. Discover what might be yours.

  1. When you're part of a group, what role do you naturally take on?
    A. The encourager who lifts others up
    B. The organizer who makes things happen
    C. The visionary who inspires fresh ideas
    D. The listener who makes people feel heard
  2. Which of these gives you the deepest sense of purpose?
    A. Helping someone in crisis
    B. Teaching or explaining a truth
    C. Creating something beautiful or meaningful
    D. Praying or interceding for others
  3. People often come to you for:
    A. Comfort and reassurance
    B. Clear advice and practical help
    C. Creative solutions or big-picture thinking
    D. Spiritual insight or encouragement
  4. When you read 1 Corinthians 12:7, what stands out to you most?
    A. “Each person is given something”
    B. “That shows who God is”
    C. “Everyone gets in on it”
    D. “Everyone benefits”
  5. You most want to reflect God’s nature as:
    A. Compassionate and healing
    B. Wise and purposeful
    C. Creative and powerful
    D. Holy and deeply personal
